# Consent and data handling

## Participant statement

This study asks you to predict small program behaviors, complete related tasks,
transfer a rule to a new example, and repair an explained error. Participation
is voluntary. You may skip a task or stop at any time without giving a reason.
Stopping withdraws your observations from the study.

The study is about the materials and language design, not your ability. There
is no individual performance report. Ask the facilitator to restate procedure,
but the facilitator cannot explain a task's semantic rule before you answer.

## Data boundary

The observation sheet records only programmer stratum, assigned condition,
task ID, coded outcome, and a coarse duration band. It does not record names,
contact details, free text, source recordings, screen recordings, voice, exact
timestamps, IP addresses, or employer. Recruitment contact data stays outside
the study package and is never joined to observations.

Raw coded sheets are deleted after two people verify the anonymous aggregate.
The retained report includes unsuccessful tasks, exclusions by predefined
reason, cohort counts, aggregate outcomes, and uncertainty. A participant may
withdraw until raw deletion is confirmed.

## Consent record

Before starting, the facilitator records only `consent: yes` on a temporary
session cover sheet. A refusal ends the session and produces no study row. The
cover sheet is destroyed with the raw coded sheet after aggregate verification.
