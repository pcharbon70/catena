# Facilitator guide

## Before a session

Confirm separate authorization for recruitment, the participant's declared
general or functional programmer stratum, the next ordinal within that stratum,
and the counterbalanced condition order produced by
`Catena.Tool.UsabilityStudy.assignment/4`. Do not run a public-language
condition while P107 or P109 is held.

Read the consent statement verbatim in meaning. Record no observation when
consent is absent. Give the same short orientation and practice item to every
participant. Explain that unfamiliar mathematics is not required and avoid
category-theory terms unless the approved future condition itself contains
them.

## During a session

Present tasks in the assigned order. Ask the participant to state a prediction
before execution, complete the requested transformation, transfer the rule to
a structurally different example, and repair a supplied diagnostic. Answer
procedural questions only. Do not reveal a rule, confirm intermediate answers,
or steer a participant toward the scoring key.

Record the predefined outcome code and duration band immediately after each
task. Preserve wrong answers and abandoned tasks. Mark a facilitator-protocol
failure only when required material was omitted or an answer was supplied.

## After a session

Ask whether the participant withdraws. Store the coded sheet separately from
recruitment records. Do not add quotations or personal notes. After the pilot,
review task ambiguity and duration, set numerical thresholds, and preregister
the frozen main-study package before collecting a main-study observation.
